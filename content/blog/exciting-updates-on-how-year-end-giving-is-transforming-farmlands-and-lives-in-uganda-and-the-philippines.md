We're thrilled to share some inspiring updates from our teams in Uganda and the Philippines—progress made possible by your generous year-end giving!

In Uganda, our new rainwater catchment and irrigation system is nearly complete.

This game-changer will boost farmland productivity, ensuring more food for our students.

Several of these tanks are being installed beside our various campus rooftops to capture rainwater which will then in turn route to a new irrigation system for our farmland.

Meanwhile, after a few delays, the soccer field on land we acquired last year is almost ready. Imagine the excitement of our high school students as they step onto their very own home field for league competitions—a dream come true!

the soccer field on land we acquired last year is almost ready

In the Philippines, at our Cebu campus, the henhouse renovation and expansion are well underway.

This major upgrade will mean healthier hens, more eggs, and better nutrition for the kids and survivors we serve.

These investments aren’t just projects—they’re lifelines that amplify the reach of our education and nutrition programs.

Thank you for rejoicing in this progress with us. Because of you, our farmland enterprises are helping orphans and survivors find brighter futures.
