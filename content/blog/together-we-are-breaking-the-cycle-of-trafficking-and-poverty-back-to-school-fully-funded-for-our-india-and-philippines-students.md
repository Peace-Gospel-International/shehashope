Thanks to the generosity of our donors, coupled with a little support from our emergency fund, we were able to fully fund our India and Philippines back to school needs for the 2024-25 school year.

This included 23 college scholarships for our graduated India orphans for the entire school year!

We just received some photos of our India students gearing up for back to school, which kicked off last week! Our students in the Philippines will be heading back to school soon.

Look, together, we did it!

Because of our donor's generosity, 293 of our students are now well-equipped to return to school in India and the Philippines!

We also have an update on our May month-end fundraiser to get our Myanmar team a new washer and dryer for the 47 children they serve on our main campus.

Our generous donors helped provide the funds needed!

Our team and the children are very happy they no longer have to wash their clothes by hand since their previous machines had recently broken down beyond repair.

Here's an old post our executive director shared in 2011 when our supporters helped us get our Myanmar team their first washing machine!

Can you imagine all the loads that machine endured?

We are so thankful for all of the ways our supporters have participated in helping us achieve these important goals...

Whether it was through their giving— now or to our emergency fund at year-end— or through their monthly giving, prayers, or emails of encouragement, we appreciate it all!
