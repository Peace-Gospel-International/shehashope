We are very happy to send you this inspiring update on the results of our year-end fundraising campaign!

Finally, all the donations dropped in the mail on 12/31 have arrived and final tallies have been calculated.

We’re happy to report that thanks to your support, encouragement, and advocacy we not only met— but exceeded— our year-end goal of $230,000!

All told, including regular recurring giving, we saw well over $250,000 in donations during the month of December!

This is a record for us. All of us at She Has Hope, globally, are humbled and grateful for the outpouring of support.

Our Executive Director, Kirby Trapolino, had a very joyous video call with our Uganda directors, Frank & Susan Tibagendeka, to inform them of the news that we will be able to fund their dream of expanding and renovating the primary school in the village of Mairinya.

They are so excited and happy that they will have their request fulfilled!

Meeting the goal not only helps our Uganda team, but will also help our India, Nepal, Myanmar, and Philippines teams as they all respond to emergencies and aid new rescues throughout the year ahead.

We are so grateful for your support and friendship in 2022, and we look forward to keeping you updated on the progress that will be possible in 2023 thanks to your generosity.
