Your support is bringing hope and opportunity to orphans, trafficking survivors, and at-risk children around the world.

Here are some quick highlights of the incredible progress we’re making together thus far in 2025!

Cebu, Philippines: New Beginnings for Girls and Growth on the Farm

Our girls home in Cebu is thriving after its recent expansion and renovation! New girls have moved in, filling the space with laughter and dreams for the future.

Meanwhile, our farmland enterprise is buzzing with activity—our new hen house is complete, and the hens are already laying eggs, boosting nutrition and income for our programs.

Cebu Girls Home: New girls moved in after the expansion and renovation was completed!

Cebu Farmland Enterprise: New hen house + new hens!

Colombia: Empowering At-Risk Girls with Skills and Love

At Casa Vida, one of our new programs in Latin America, our Director Isabelina is changing lives by mentoring at-risk teen refugee girls, keeping them safe from the very real threat of human trafficking in the region.

Her trade school teaches seamstress skills, offering a path to independence for young refugee women and survivors of crisis.

Beyond training, Isabelina provides love and hope, even celebrating milestones like quinceañeras for the girls. Your generosity is giving these young women a fresh start!

Casa Vida, Colombia: Isabelina providing a quinceañera for one of the girls enrolled in the program

Colombia “Casa Vida” Trade School: At-risk refugee girls enrolled in the program receive seamstress training

Uganda: A Beautiful Campus and Sustainable Future

Our primary school campus in Uganda is taking shape as a stunning, spacious place for our 567 students to learn and grow.

During my visit last month, one detail I loved seeing was that the teachers have added their own touch, creating vibrant illustrations to hang on the walls!

Plus, our new rainwater harvest and irrigation system was super impressive to see — it’s ensuring sustainable farming, supporting our orphans, students, and the community.

Uganda Primary School: The teachers made all of the illustrations you see on the walls!

Uganda Farmland Enterprise: New rainwater harvest and irrigation system

Nepal: A Safe Haven for New Beginnings

Our rehabilitation home and trade school in Nepal welcomed a new group of girls in March. They’re settling in, diving into coursework, and beaming with excitement for their future.

Your support is providing them a safe place to heal and build skills for a brighter future.

Nepal Rehabilitation Home & Trade School: They are so happy to be enrolled at the home

From new homes to thriving trade schools, your giving is creating lasting change by the grace of God.

Thank you for standing with us to lift up orphans and trafficking survivors worldwide!

None of this would be possible without you.
